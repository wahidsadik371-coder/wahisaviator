# Global Flagship Smartphone Census — cutoff 14 October 2026

**Purpose:** a factual reference on flagship smartphones. Not a ranking, not a review.

---

## 1. How to read this document — read this first

Every factual row carries a **Verified** tag. It answers one question only: *did I open the source document and read it, or did I get the information from someone reporting about it?*

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | I fetched the document itself and read it. The claim is traceable to text I actually retrieved. |
| **B** | The claim comes from a search summary or a news article **about** the source. I did not open the source. It may well be correct, but **this census has not verified it.** |
| **A+B** | Some cited sources are first-hand, some are not. |
| — | No source attached, or the value is `Undisclosed`. |

**This distinction is the most important thing in the document.** Nothing tagged **B** should be quoted as established fact. It is reported material awaiting verification.

## 3. Scope rules

- **Cutoff:** 14 October 2026.

- **Current flagship:** officially announced, released for sale in at least one market, and still actively marketed as a current flagship on the cutoff date.

- **Excluded:** teasers, leaks, certification-only devices, announced-but-unreleased products, rumoured devices, and prior-generation devices.

- **Sub-brands are folded into their parent** so nothing is double-counted: Redmi/POCO → Xiaomi; iQOO/realme → BBK; nubia/REDMAGIC → ZTE; CMF → Nothing. HONOR is its own parent (independent since the 2020 spinoff).

## 19. Errata — defects found and corrected

A referential-integrity and provenance audit was run against the finished dataset. It found four defects, all corrected and preserved here rather than silently overwritten.

| # | Defect | Severity | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Apple model codes assigned by inference, and self-contradictory.** A3472 was labelled the Pro and A3473 the Pro Max, both tagged '(US)' — although Apple's page states no such mapping, and A3473 was simultaneously described as lacking the US band B106. | High | Both recorded as an **unmapped family pair**; the '(US)' attribution withdrawn. **The pattern.** Defects 1 and 3 are the same failure: a plausible-looking identifier filled a field that should have read `Undisclosed`. Both were caught by mechanical cross-checks, not by re-reading. The build now runs those checks automatically and reports zero cross-brand contamination.

## 20. Limitations


1. **Most of this document is unverified.** 55 of 67 sources were not opened. Section 14 is where that lands. Read every **B** tag as "reported, not checked".

2. **This is not "all phones worldwide."** It is every eligible device found under the documented method. Manufacturer discovery ran through Wikipedia's country-sorted brand list plus local-language search; GSMArena's phone-finder endpoint returns 404, so no structured phone database could be swept. Sony, Sharp, Fairphone, HONOR, TECNO/Infinix and the regional vendors have a register disposition but no dossier.

3. **Absent values are labelled, not filled.** Apple publishes no iPhone RAM figure. OPPO and vivo do not name camera sensors. vivo publishes no display brightness. Samsung's own spec page does not name the S26 Ultra's chipset. All of these read `Undisclosed` rather than carrying a plausible guess.

4. **Two figures are known to disagree.** The OPPO Find X9 Ultra's GSMArena endurance appears as both 20h 09m and 21h 57m; both are recorded and the conflict is flagged rather than averaged. DXOMARK's sensor figures for the Xiaomi 17 Ultra differ from Xiaomi's own.

5. **The highest-value next step is verification, not expansion.** Opening the primary pages behind the 55 Tier B sources — iFixit's teardowns and GSMArena's battery pages first — would convert reported material into verified material. Adding more devices on an unverified base would compound the problem rather than solve it.
